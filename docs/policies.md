# Northstar Goods — fictional policies (v1)

All details are fictional. The app loads the canonical policy IDs and text from `data/policies.json` for both demo and live prompts. Staff must review responses; no action is performed automatically.

| ID | Supplied policy |
| --- | --- |
| returns | Unworn goods may be returned within 30 days of delivery; staff must verify eligibility. |
| refunds | Eligible refunds typically take 5–10 business days after receipt and inspection. |
| orders | Confirm the order reference and investigate tracking before promising a replacement. |
| general | Staff can clarify shipping and product questions; do not invent unsupported details. |
| access | Use the official password reset process. Never request passwords or verification codes. |
| security | Unauthorized account changes or account use require security review. Never automatically change an account. |
| phishing | Do not click suspicious links or share credentials. A suspicious email does not prove a breach. |
