3. Understand the human decision

In the staff workspace, an authorized reviewer can approve, edit, or reject a recommendation and record a rationale. Customer-service staff see ordinary requests; security staff see security tickets. Actions are audited.

This public sandbox is read-only and uses only the eight bundled fictional scenarios. It does not expose private staff tickets, permit imports, store visitor messages, or call a paid AI service. Staff controls require a private account.
